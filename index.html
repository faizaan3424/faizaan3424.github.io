LifeOps

A private personal application operated by Faizaan Aslam to organize everyday information and carry out actions he requests.

What LifeOps does

LifeOps connects the owner's chosen services to a private automation system. Its YouTube integration can search for videos, read the owner's playlists and subscriptions, create playlists, add videos to playlists, and subscribe to channels when requested.

Why Google access is requested

Google OAuth lets LifeOps perform supported actions for the Google or YouTube account that explicitly authorizes it. Permissions are shown on Google's consent screen. Access can be revoked at any time in Google Account connections.

Personal use

LifeOps is intended for its owner's personal use. This website provides application information and a privacy policy; it does not offer public registration or a dashboard.

LifeOps is an independent application and is not endorsed by Google or YouTube.
