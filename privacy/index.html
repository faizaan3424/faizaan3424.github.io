Privacy policy

Effective October 12, 2026

This policy describes the Google and YouTube integration of LifeOps, a personal application operated by Faizaan Aslam. Contact: faizaan3424@gmail.com.

Data accessed and collected

LifeOps uses Google OAuth and YouTube API Services. Depending on the permissions granted and the action requested, it accesses YouTube search results, video and channel identifiers and metadata, the authorized account's playlists and playlist items (including private playlists), and subscriptions. It receives OAuth authorization credentials needed to access the account. It does not ask for or store the Google Account password.

How data is used

Data is used to provide the owner's requested features: finding videos, viewing and organizing playlists, creating playlists, adding videos, and managing subscriptions. LifeOps does not sell Google or YouTube data or use it for advertising, credit decisions, or training generalized AI models.

Storage and protection

OAuth credentials are held in the private LifeOps deployment using Google Cloud Secret Manager. YouTube API responses are processed by the private application and returned to the owner or an AI client the owner has connected. The public website and its public repository do not contain OAuth credentials or private account data.

The current YouTube adapter reads API data on demand rather than maintaining a separate YouTube metadata database. Operational records and conversations may be retained by the private deployment and the connected clients. Private operational backups can have separate retention periods; revoking access does not automatically erase those records.

Sharing and connected services

Google and YouTube receive requests needed to provide the integration. Google Cloud hosts the private LifeOps services and credential storage. When the owner invokes LifeOps through a connected AI client, the requested results are sent to that client and handled under its own privacy and retention settings. Human access is limited to the owner and access needed for security, legal obligations, or an explicitly authorized support purpose. LifeOps does not make private YouTube data public.

Retention, deletion, and control

OAuth credentials are kept while the integration is enabled and are replaced or removed when the owner disconnects or requests deletion. To stop future access, revoke LifeOps in Google Account connections. To request removal of stored LifeOps credentials or API data, email faizaan3424@gmail.com. Authorized YouTube API data is to be deleted as soon as possible and within 7 days of a deletion request or revocation as required by YouTube's policies. A deletion request is handled by the operator; this website is not an automated deletion tool.

Playlists and other changes saved to YouTube remain in the YouTube account until changed or deleted there. Removing LifeOps access does not delete those playlists. Manage data held in connected AI clients through those clients' controls.

Google API limited use

LifeOps' use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube and Google policies

Use of YouTube features is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can review or revoke LifeOps access through Google's security permissions page.

This public website

This site uses no application analytics, advertising, login form, or tracking scripts. GitHub Pages hosts it and may process technical request data under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Changes

The operator will update this page and its effective date when these practices change. Questions and privacy requests can be sent to faizaan3424@gmail.com.
